Who We Are

We’re just a small group of friends who love the old internet — the era of glossy gradients, floating clouds, and hopeful start-up sounds that made the web feel alive.

This site is our little restoration project — a tribute to when technology felt magical, communities were personal, and creativity flowed freely through dial-up tones and Dreamweaver windows.

Whether you found us by accident or nostalgia, you’re part of our web revival now. Welcome back to the Internet — restored.
